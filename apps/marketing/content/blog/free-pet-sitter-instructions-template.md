# Free Pet Sitter Instructions Template

Writing a handover from scratch every time is how things get left out. Here's a template you can copy, fill in once, and hand to anyone looking after your pet — print it, save it as a note, whatever's fastest for you right now.

## The template

**Pet basics**
- Name, species, breed, age, weight
- Microchip number
- Any distinguishing markings (useful if they ever go missing)

**Feeding**
- Food type and brand
- Amount and times
- Treats — allowed / not allowed
- Water — where, how often refreshed

**Routine**
- Walks: how long, how often, on/off lead
- Toileting: where, how often
- Sleep: where, any quirks

**Medical**
- Vet name, clinic, phone
- After-hours/emergency vet
- Medications: what, dose, when, how administered
- Allergies or conditions
- Written permission to seek emergency treatment
- Spending limit before they need to check with you
- Backup decision-maker if you're unreachable

**Behaviour and quirks**
- Commands they respond to
- Known triggers
- Anything that looks weird but is completely normal for them
- How they are with strangers, kids, other animals

**House logistics**
- Where things live (food, leads, litter, meds)
- Off-limits areas
- Keys, alarm code, parking

**Contact**
- Your number and best way to reach you
- How often you'd like updates
- What counts as a "call me now" situation

## How to use it

Fill it in once when things are stable, then update it before every stay rather than trusting last time's version is still accurate — medications change, vets change, routines change. That's the part a static template can't do for you, and it's the exact gap Quirks & All is built to close: the same information, but it updates the moment something changes and stays current on the link you share, instead of quietly going stale in a doc nobody reopens.
